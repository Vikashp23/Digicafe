Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026

This policy explains how DigiCafe (“we”, “us”) handles information you provide when you visit our website, submit an enquiry, apply for an opportunity or subscribe to updates.

1. Information you may provide

Depending on the service, this may include your name, email address, mobile number, business or service details, documents you choose to submit, and information needed to respond to your request. Newsletter subscriptions may collect your email address, name and consent to receive updates.

2. How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and provide requested assistance.
  • To contact you about an application, service request or collaboration.
  • To send newsletters or updates where you have opted in.
  • To maintain records, improve the website and protect against misuse.

3. Sharing and third-party services

We do not offer personal information for sale. Information may be processed by service providers you interact with through this website, such as Google Forms, Google Sheets, email, hosting or government portals. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. We may disclose information where required by law or necessary to protect rights and security.

4. Documents and sensitive information

Submit only information reasonably required for your request. Do not send passwords, OTPs, card PINs or unrelated sensitive information. Use official government portals for credentials and authentication wherever possible.

5. Retention and security

We take reasonable steps to limit access and protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Information is retained only as reasonably needed for the purpose collected, operational records or legal obligations.

6. Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete information we hold, subject to applicable record-keeping requirements. You can stop newsletter emails by using the unsubscribe instructions provided in the email or contacting us directly.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email digicafe.admin@gmail.com.

This page is a general privacy notice and may be updated as DigiCafe services and processes change.
